## The one `OBSERVATION_DOES_NOT_EXIST`

**Resident receipt of warning.** Korean CBS (Cell Broadcast Service) is a
one-to-many broadcast to handsets in a cell area. The architecture has **no
return path** — it is not a messaging system that confirms delivery, and no
per-recipient receipt is generated by any party.

This is an affirmative argument from how the system is built, not an inference
from a failed retrieval. It is the only gap in this table that earns the
designation. Every other gap is either obtained, gated, or not yet retrieved.

- **3 are `PARTIALLY_AVAILABLE`**.
- **3 are `NOT_RETRIEVED`** or `UNKNOWN` — blocked by bot-verification walls or

**No quantity in this table is marked absent on the strength of a failed
request.** Three GK2A slots initially returned `SERVER_ERROR` and all three
returned the product on re-probe; recording them as first seen would have put
three false absences into this registry.

3. **`KMA_API_KEY`** (apihub.kma.go.kr) — the GK2A `FF`/`DQF_FF` NetCDF, turning
   observation *availability* into observation *content*.

   alert records already harvested, sharpening geography from prose to codes.
   Requires SMS verification of a Korean mobile number.
5. **산불통계연보 2025 PDF** — the definitive retrospective per-incident record;
   needs a browser context.
